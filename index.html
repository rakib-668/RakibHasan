01 — About

Who I am

I'm a Computer Science & Technology student at Sylhet Polytechnic Institute, currently based in Bogura, Bangladesh. My long-term goal is to work as an ethical hacker — someone who finds weaknesses in systems in order to help fix them, under proper authorization, not exploit them.

I'm building that path deliberately: starting with Linux fundamentals and networking rather than jumping straight to tools, then layering practical skills like network reconnaissance with Nmap on top of that foundation.

Alongside security, I'm also exploring how different AI models work and how to prompt them effectively — a skill set I think will matter more, not less, in the security field going forward.
